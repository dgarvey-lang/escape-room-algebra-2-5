The Ghost's Spellbook

Breaking a radical lock

Spell 1 · Isolate

Get the squared part alone. Undo the + or − first, then divide by the number in front.

2(x − 1)2 + 4 = 10  →  (x − 1)2 = 3
Spell 2 · Root with ±

Take the square root of both sides. Always write ±, because there are two answers.

3x2 = 5  →  x2 = 53  →  x = ±√53
Spell 3 · Split the root

The root of a fraction is the root of the top over the root of the bottom.

√53 = √5√3
Spell 4 · Rationalize

A radical can't stay in the denominator. Multiply the top and bottom by that same radical. The bottom becomes a whole number because √3 · √3 = 3.

√5√3 · √3√3 = √153
Spell 5 · Simplify and reduce

Pull perfect squares out of the radical, then reduce the fraction if the numbers share a factor.

√124 = 2√34 = √32
Crypt spell · Negative under the root

If the number under the root is negative, pull out √(−1) = i, then keep going.

√−53 = i√53 = i√153
Typing tips

√15/3 means √15 over 3. Write 2 + √15/3 or (6 + √15)/3. Write i√15/3 for imaginary answers. Type ± once to fill both boxes.
